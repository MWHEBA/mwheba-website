## Current stock and quantity rules

- **Stock** is excluded from the current MWHEBA scope by Decision D-018, including stock counts, inventory gating, and warehouse tracking. Payload Ecommerce is configured with `inventory: false` in [payload.config.ts](../../src/payload.config.ts#L114-L115). The reviewed paths do not provide stock reservations or adjustments. The product page's JSON-LD `inStock` value describes sample display data only.
- **Physical product MOQ** follows Decision D-009, which specifies 10 units per physical product aggregated across its variants. The current code treats 10 as a configurable floor. Configured values above 10 are supported. Fixed-price services use MOQ 1.
- **Cart edits** accept positive integer quantities below MOQ. The system checks variant ownership, aggregates quantities across variants of the same product, and keeps quantities for different products separate. An incomplete cart exposes the remaining quantity. Checkout validation returns HTTP 422 for an under-MOQ cart. Order creation revalidates the cart before its transaction.
- **Test coverage** shows that 5 + 5 units across variants of one product meet the MOQ, while quantities split across two products do not. Tests also cover service MOQ 1. This is domain-level test evidence. End-to-end storefront checkout remains unverified.
- **Storefront data** currently comes from sample products. Product detail pages submit sample product/variant IDs to cart code that resolves Payload records. End-to-end compatibility of those identities remains unverified.

## Legacy MOQ consistency gap

Static source review found inconsistent handling for physical products without persisted `minOrderQuantity`. Cart quote and hydration resolve them to MOQ 10. Order creation resolves them to MOQ 1. The create-order API calls that service path directly. Both server paths honor persisted physical MOQ values above 10. The product page's quantity label is hardcoded to 10, while its quantity stepper uses the product's `minMoq` value. Adding a new cart line with quantity zero returns HTTP 422. Patching an existing line to quantity zero removes it. These are pre-existing code-path findings from source review.

## Changes that require a decision first

The commerce-platform plan proposes stock per sellable SKU and configurable physical-product MOQ down to one, while retaining existing B2B rules. Those proposals change D-018 and D-009. Record and approve the updated scope and quantity policy before inventory schema or migration work. Until then, stock remains out of scope and 10 remains the physical MOQ floor. Persisted physical MOQ values above 10 are supported. Online payments remain deferred under D-008. Current payment methods are COD and manual bank transfer.
