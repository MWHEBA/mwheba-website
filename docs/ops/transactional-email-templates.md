# MWHEBA 2.0 | Transactional Email Templates Runbook

> **Target Module:** `src/shared/lib/emailTemplates.ts`  
> **Status:** Implemented & Tested (`tests/emailTemplates.test.ts`)  
> **Design Constraint:** Calming, minimal, flat colors, no CSS gradients, bulletproof table markup for Outlook, Gmail, and Apple Mail across Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR).

---

## 1. Overview & Operational Principles

All automated client communications in MWHEBA 2.0 adhere to the following principles:
1. **Server-Authoritative Data:** Monetary totals, IBAN numbers, order statuses, and proof links are generated by server logic; templates never accept unvalidated client inputs.
2. **Review Mode Safety:** Live email dispatch requires explicit operational enablement (`enableRealDispatch: true` in `inquiry-settings` and real SMTP credentials). During development and review modes, emails are logged to console or recorded in database records with status `skipped_review_mode`.
3. **Cross-Client Layout Stability:** HTML emails use inline styles and nested tables with MSO conditional tags for rendering in legacy Microsoft Outlook desktop clients without layout breaks.

## 2. Catalog of Standard Transactional Templates

| Template ID | Event Trigger | Recipient | Key Included Metadata |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lead_notification` | Corporate lead form submitted | `sales@mwheba.com` (Agency Sales) | Client name, company, email, phone, requested service, estimated budget, project details. |
| `order_confirmation` | B2B checkout completed | Customer | Order number, customer name, frozen final total (EGP), payment instructions (CIB bank transfer IBAN or COD courier notice). |
| `proof_ready` | Artwork proof uploaded by production | Customer | Order number, product title, secure customer portal link for online review & approval gate. |
| `bank_transfer_cleared` | Wire verified by finance team | Customer | Order number, verified amount in EGP, confirmation that production lines have commenced. |
